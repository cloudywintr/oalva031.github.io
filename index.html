Maya Torres

Business Administration Student 📚

Hi, I'm Maya Torres, a Business Administration student at the University of California, Riverside, with a concentration in Marketing. I grew up in the Inland Empire watching my family run a small neighborhood bakery, and those early mornings behind the counter taught me more about business than any textbook could. I learned how much a friendly greeting matters, how quickly inventory can turn into waste if nobody is paying attention, and how a single loyal customer can bring in ten more. Those lessons are a big part of why I chose to study business, and they still shape the way I think about every project I take on.

At UCR, my coursework has focused on marketing strategy, consumer behavior, business analytics, and operations management. I enjoy classes where I get to work with real data and turn it into decisions, whether that means building a pricing model in Excel, running a customer survey, or putting together a marketing plan for a local business. I have also taken introductory computer science courses, including CS8, where I learned the basics of HTML, CSS, and web design. Building this website is part of that work, and it has shown me how valuable it is for business professionals to understand the technology their companies rely on. I like being the person on a team who can talk with both the numbers people and the creative people and help them find common ground.

Outside the classroom, I have worked in retail and customer service, served as the marketing chair for a student business club, and volunteered with a local food bank, where I helped organize donation drives and coordinate volunteer schedules. Each of these experiences has strengthened my communication, organization, and problem-solving skills. In retail, I learned how to stay calm and helpful during the busiest rush of the holiday season. In my club role, I learned how to plan events from start to finish, manage a small budget, and grow attendance through social media campaigns. At the food bank, I saw firsthand how good logistics and clear communication can make a real difference in people's lives.

My professional interests include digital marketing, brand strategy, and helping small businesses grow. I am especially interested in how social media, data analytics, and customer experience come together to build strong brands. After graduation, I hope to work in a marketing or business development role where I can combine creativity with analysis, and eventually I would love to consult for small and family-owned businesses like the one I grew up in. I believe that small businesses are the heart of every community, and I want to help them compete and thrive.

When I am not studying or working, you can usually find me trying new coffee shops, hiking the trails around Mount Rubidoux, reading business and leadership books, or experimenting with new recipes in the kitchen. I am always open to connecting with students, professionals, and mentors, so feel free to reach out through any of the links below. Thanks for stopping by my website!
